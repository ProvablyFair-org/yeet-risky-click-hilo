YEET's Discord, checked 23 November 2026
========================================

What we checked
- #announcements: the 18 posts from 5 to 21 November 2026 (UTC), the last
  posts before the check.
- #patch-notes: the posts from 15 September to 13 November 2026, plus the
  4 September 2026 patch notes.
- Server search: hilo, hi-lo, risky, rtp, "house edge", refund,
  compensation, compound, "per card", "per click" and migrated.
  search-log.json lists every query, the results Discord showed and how
  many we read.

What we found
- No post explains the HiLo or Risky Click pricing change, and no post
  announces refunds or compensation for it.
- YEET's 13 November patch notes are the first after the change. They list
  bet cards going live, the live win feed pausing on hover, a renamed
  promotions row, badge alignment and email footers. Nothing about HiLo or
  Risky Click pricing. They were forwarded to #announcements the same
  evening.
- On 27 June 2025 a YEET team member told players in #general that the YEET
  originals are 99% RTP, Risky Click included. Nothing about the edge
  growing with each tile.
- The 4 September 2026 patch notes say Risky Click moved to a new game
  engine to run faster. Nothing about pricing.

Limits
Message links open only for members of YEET's Discord. Search can miss
edited, deleted or unindexed posts, and we did not open every image or
link. For the broad searches (rtp, compound and unfiltered hi-lo) we read
the first page of results. We cannot see emails, private messages or
account credits.
